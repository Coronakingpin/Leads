# Text message: owners with a phone but no email

Merge file: `output/text-campaign.csv` (built after email research finishes).
Keep it under 320 characters (2 SMS segments). No links in the first text.

---

**Text 1**

Hi {{first_name}}, this is [Your Name] with Outta The Madness, a local Louisville repair crew. Metro's report on {{property_address}} lists {{repair_items}}. We can usually clear items like that within the week. Want a free price? Reply STOP to opt out.

**Follow-up (3 days later, only if no reply and no STOP)**

Hi {{first_name}}, [Your Name] again with Outta The Madness. Still happy to price the work at {{property_address}}. Text back or call (502) 808-2060. Reply STOP to opt out.

---

## Rules (legal, read before sending)

- **Send each text by hand from your phone or a 1:1 business texting app. Don't use a bulk/auto-text blaster.** Under the TCPA, automated marketing texts to cell phones without prior written consent are $500–$1,500 *per text*.
- Honor STOP immediately and keep a do-not-contact list.
- Only text 8am–9pm Louisville time.
- Office/landline numbers can't receive texts. Call those instead.
- Never say or imply the text is from Louisville Metro.
