Common questions

FAQ

What areas do you serve?

All of Louisville Metro and Jefferson County, KY. We're mobile: we come to your property, so there's no shop to visit.

Can you fix a Louisville Metro code violation before the reinspection?

Yes. Send us the notice or the listed items and the reinspection date. We price each item, do the work, and send before-and-after photos for your records. We're a private company, not affiliated with Louisville Metro Government.

Do you do make-ready turnovers between tenants?

Yes. Move-out cleaning, trash-out, paint touch-ups, drywall patches, fixture swaps and small repairs so the unit is ready to show and rent.

What work don't you do?

We don't do HVAC, electrical panels or wiring, gas lines, roofing or structural work. For those we'll tell you to call a licensed specialist.

How do I get a quote?

Call or text (502) 808-2060, or fill out the quote form with the property address and what needs doing. Photos help us price it faster.
